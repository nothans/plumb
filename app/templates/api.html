API

Everything the web pages do, as JSON. Authenticate with Authorization: Bearer <token> (make one on your page) or your session cookie. The OpenAPI 3 document is at /api/openapi.json. Errors are {"error": "..."} with a meaningful status: 401 not logged in, 403 not allowed, 404 not found, 409 not now (a deadline or a lock), 422 invalid input, 429 slow down.

Webhooks

An organizer can register a URL per event. Every audited action in that event (submissions, reviews, assignments, votes, publication, and the rest) is POSTed to it in order, as JSON, with X-Plumb-Signature: sha256=<hex HMAC-SHA256 of the body with the webhook secret>. Failed deliveries retry with backoff.
